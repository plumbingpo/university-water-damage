When to Hire a Water Damage Professional in University, FL

Home / Blog / When to Hire a Water Damage Professional in University, FL

After a leak or flood at a house in University, FL, you have to decide quickly between doing the cleanup yourself and hiring professionals. Most water losses turn out to be more involved than they look, even though handling them yourself seems like the cheaper route. Below we explain which jobs a homeowner can reasonably take on and which ones need a professional crew.

Jobs You May Be Able to Handle Yourself

Very small, contained water events may be manageable without professional help. Say a sink overflows with clean water, wets a hard, non-porous floor over an area smaller than about 10 square feet, and is cleaned up inside of an hour: that is probably a job for you. Similarly, a small spill on tile or vinyl that is mopped up immediately, with the area dried by fans and a dehumidifier, probably does not require professional help. What matters is a small affected area, a clean source, a fast cleanup, and no significant wetting of anything porous.

When to Call a Restoration Company

Most of the water damage people actually face is beyond a do-it-yourself fix. Do not wait to call if any of these apply: a musty odor that points to early mold, a source you are unable to locate or stop, possible water in wall cavities or framing, contaminated water (black or gray), wet drywall, carpet, or similar porous materials, several hours of standing water, or a wet area bigger than 10 square feet.

What DIY Cleanup Can Really Cost

Many University homeowners who attempt DIY water damage cleanup end up spending more than they would have with professional restoration. The first reason is mold, which grows within days when drying is incomplete and adds $1,500 to $6,000 or more for remediation. Damaged materials that are not properly dried deteriorate further, increasing replacement costs. The cost of rented extraction and drying gear, fans and dehumidifiers included, mounts quickly while performing below a professional setup. If the cleanup is poorly documented, your insurance payout may be smaller. Finally, every day lost to a cleanup that is not working pushes real restoration back and lets the damage grow.

What a Professional Crew Brings

A professional crew brings things a homeowner cannot: extraction from a truck-mounted unit, hundreds of times faster at removing water, commercial dryers able to reach structural materials and the cavities inside walls, hidden moisture found by thermal imaging, readings that confirm drying is complete, an antimicrobial treatment to help keep mold from growing, full records to back up an insurance claim, and confidence that the work was done properly.
