How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
  • Going away longer than a few days? Close the main water valve first.
  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.

Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • A growing pool on the floor is heading for the next room or running downstairs.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
