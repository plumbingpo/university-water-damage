Sump Pump Failure Cleanup Help in University

The moment a basement most needs its sump pump is often the moment the pump gives out. Groundwater rises in a big storm and pours into the pit, right as the electricity fails, the float jams or the inflow outruns the pump. From there it tops the edge of the basin and runs out over the slab. Nothing stops it until some pump is working again.

This water is not clean. Soil, silt, bacteria and the contents of the drain tile travel with it, so our team rates it no better than Category 2, and Category 3 when it has stood for a while or sewage is involved. For the job, this means soaked porous items such as drywall and carpet pad are usually discarded, and what stays gets washed and disinfected first, then dried.

Right Now

Your First Steps

  1. Stay Out of the WaterNever wade into basement water before the power has been cut. Water may be touching outlets, the furnace or the pump's own cord.
  2. Turn Off the Basement CircuitsBasement breakers should be flipped off, provided you can get to the panel without standing in water. If the panel is in the water, call the utility.
  3. Look at the Pump From a DistanceLook to see whether the pump came unplugged, its outlet tripped or the float is pinned against the pit wall. Keep your hands out of the water.
  4. Treat the Water as ContaminatedIf going down cannot be avoided, put on gloves and rubber boots, leave pets and children upstairs, and wash up after handling wet items.
  5. Save What Is Dry, Then CallTake pictures of the pump and the water level, bring valuables that are still dry upstairs, and phone us. Do not wait for the water to drain away by itself.
How We Fix It

Our Sump Pump Failure Cleanup Process

1

Get the Water Out

Our crew arrives with extractors and pumps, which means your dead sump is not needed. Standing water and silt are removed from the floor, the pit area and any low spots.

2

Take Out What Got Wet

Our team wears protective gear to remove wet drywall, insulation, carpet and pad reached by groundwater, then bags and hauls it off, leaving bare framing and concrete.

3

Wash and Disinfect

We wash the framing, the slab and the contents that are staying, then apply an antimicrobial. Doing it ahead of drying keeps the air movers from spreading contaminants through the basement.

4

Dry and Verify

We run drying equipment until our meters show normal moisture in the slab and the wood, which typically takes 3 to 5 days. Before we pull our equipment, the pit should have a pump that works.

The Usual Culprits

How It Usually Starts

  • The float getting caught on the side of the basin or tangled in its cord.
  • A tripped outlet or breaker, or a plug knocked loose, leaving the pump without power.
  • Motor failure in a pump that has simply reached the end of its life.
  • Losing electricity in a storm when the pump has no battery backup.
  • A pump too small for the volume of water entering the pit.
If It Is Left

Where the Damage Spreads

Finished Basement Walls

Drywall and insulation wick dirty water upward from the slab, well above the visible water line. Since it is contaminated water, we remove the wet bottom portion instead of drying it.

Furnace and Water Heater

Mechanical equipment often shares the floor with the sump pit. Water reaching burners, controls or wiring can ruin them, and each appliance must be inspected before it is used again.

Bottom Plates and Studs

Framing lumber at floor level takes on groundwater before anything else in the wall. Once the drywall is off, the plates and studs are washed, disinfected and dried.

Basement Floor Coverings

Carpet and pad soaked by groundwater cannot be cleaned to a safe standard and are removed. Vinyl plank and laminate floors generally have to be pulled as well, since dirty water stays under them.

Insurance

Insurance and Sump Pump Failure Cleanup

This is one of the most misunderstood claims. A standard homeowners policy usually excludes water that backs up or overflows from a sump, and flood insurance is a separate policy with its own rules. Unless you bought an endorsement for sump overflow or water backup there is often no coverage, and such endorsements are capped. Your declarations page will list it, or your agent can tell you. We record the pump failure, the water level and the damage so your claim file is complete.
