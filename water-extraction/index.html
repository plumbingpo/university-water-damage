Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. Across University, University Restoration Co handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • Seams in the floor that ooze water under foot pressure
  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets
  • Rising water in the basement because the sump pump stopped working
  • Water accumulating after a fire suppression system discharges
  • Water visible in crawl spaces, mechanical rooms or utility areas
  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Add our number, (801) 306-8232, to your cell so help is one tap away in a water emergency
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails
  • Raise anything you store in the basement a few inches above the floor
  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number
  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule

Why Professional Water Extraction Matters

The speed and thoroughness of water extraction directly determine the extent of damage to your property and the cost of restoration. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. It follows plumbing lines and electrical conduits, slips between flooring layers, climbs drywall by wicking and is drawn into carpet pad through capillary action. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.
